The ladder of matter: atoms build everything. One kind of atom = an ELEMENT. Atoms bonded together = a MOLECULE (O₂ is a molecule of an element; H₂O is a molecule of a compound). Different elements bonded = a COMPOUND. No bonding, just mingling = a MIXTURE — same throughout (homogeneous) or visibly chunky (heterogeneous). Elements and compounds are the only PURE substances.

7. A block has a density of 0.7 g/mL. Drop it in water — what happens?
Water's density is 1 g/mL. Less dense than water → floats. Denser → sinks. 0.7 < 1, so it rides high with about 70% of it underwater. This one number — 1 g/mL — is the referee for every float/sink question.
